# Canvas & Gestures

- **Rotate to draw comfortably.** Long, confident strokes are easier at a natural
  wrist angle - rotate the canvas with **Shift + pan-drag** instead of twisting
  your hand, and hold **Ctrl** to snap.
- **Reset the view quickly.** Use the rotator dial in the bottom info bar to snap
  back to upright when you lose track of the angle.
- **Mirror with symmetry guides.** For faces, mechs and mandalas, turn on a
  symmetry guide and paint one side - the other side mirrors live.
- **History is saved.** You can close a document and still undo recent steps
  after reopening it, so don't be afraid to experiment.
