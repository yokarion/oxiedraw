#title

OxieDraw is a fast, clean drawing app for Linux and other desktops - a simple, yet powerful tool, built in Rust and GPU-accelerated with Vulkan.

#features
  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-zap
  ---
  #title
  Vulkan-accelerated

  #description
  A Vulkan rendering pipeline keeps strokes low-latency, without spinning up your CPU 100% and utilizing the best property of your GPU - drawing images fast.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-layers
  ---
  #title
  Layers, groups and blend modes

  #description
  Full layer stack with folders, per-layer blend modes and opacity, composited on the GPU.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-sliders-horizontal
  ---
  #title
  Non-destructive adjustments

  #description
  Adjustment layers for Hue/Saturation, Blur and Stroke with a painted mask and live preview.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-brush
  ---
  #title
  Advanced brush engine

  #description
  Parameter-driven brushes. Use most common brushes from Krita, ProCreate and GIMP out of the box.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-type
  ---
  #title
  Text and typography

  #description
  Figma-like text tool with per-range styling and embedded fonts.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-scan-line
  ---
  #title
  Selections, masks and guides

  #description
  Shapes, selections, masks, gestures and symmetry drawing guides.
  :::
