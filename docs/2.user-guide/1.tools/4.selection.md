# Selection

Selections constrain painting, filling and filters to a region of the canvas.

## Shapes

- **Rectangular** - drag a box.
- **Elliptical** - drag an ellipse.
- **Freehand** - draw an arbitrary outline.

Hold `Shift` to add to the selection, `Alt` to subtract, both to intersect.
With nothing selected yet, `Shift` constrains the drag to a square or circle.

## Mask editing

Paint the selection instead of drawing a shape. Size is shared with the Brush;
Strength follows pen pressure and builds up while you hold the brush in place.

- **Marquee** - drag the shape above. Shows the shape it will draw.
- **Add** - paint selection in.
- **Erase** - paint selection out.
- **Blur** - soften the mask under the brush.

## Mask Preview

Tints the canvas by coverage - blue barely selected, red fully selected,
unselected left clear.

::note
Outline marks the >0% line, so a feathered or half-erased selection
looks identical to a hard one. Mask Preview is how you see the difference.
::

## Working with a selection

While a selection is active, every edit is clipped to it. Selections pair well
with masks and adjustment layers - see
[Layers & Compositing](/user-guide/layers-and-compositing) - and can be turned
into a layer mask to make the clip permanent and re-editable.
